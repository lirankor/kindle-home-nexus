- Keep the home-control experience in the index route with in-page device tabs, because all four views share a fixed e-ink screen.
- Use semantic grayscale tokens and the eink Button variant for controls, because focus and state must remain visible without color or animation.
- Home Assistant is reached only through server functions in `src/lib/` (`getSnapshot`, `runAction`, `getScreensaver`); the entity map and the optimistic reducer live in `src/lib/home.ts`. HA/Immich env vars are read server-side from `process.env` and must never reach the browser.
- Without `HA_TOKEN` the UI runs on demo data and the status line says "Demo · …"; with it, show the real action result or error ("HA unreachable") and never imply success when HA failed. Optimistic state is reverted on failure.
- The photo screensaver wakes on the first input without activating an underlying control; the bundled sample imagery is labeled until `IMMICH_API_KEY` is set, then a random Immich favorite is shown.
- Soft keys: the bridge delivers the Kindle's four bottom buttons as keyboard keys `F1`..`F4` (Back, Keyboard, Menu, Home, left to right). The footer (`DeviceActions`) always has four slots mapped 1:1 to them in every context, main tabs and modals alike. Pressing `F1`..`F4` runs the footer action of that slot (`pressSoftKey`); unused slots are blank disabled buttons so positions stay stable. Footer buttons stay focusable and clickable. Keep the footer in sync with the key handler: one `actions` array feeds both.
- Keys: PageUp/PageDown switch tabs, arrows move focus, Enter activates, Escape closes. While a modal is open the arrows and PageUp/PageDown stop navigating and act on the modal instead (light modal: Up/Right/PageDown = brightness +10, Down/Left/PageUp = -10; pickers: arrows move the selection and apply it immediately, Enter closes).
- Light modals (`src/components/light-modals.tsx`) are full 600x800 pages with no backdrop: the light modal (Enter on a light card; footer Close, Colour / White, On / Off, Warm / Cool) and the white-shade and colour pickers (footer Close, Back). Action payloads keep the English HA names. Every modal closes after 30 s without a key press and when the screensaver starts.
- E-ink refresh hint: `requestFullRefresh()` in `src/lib/eink.ts` sets `<html data-eink-refresh="full">` for about 1.5 s, then back to `partial`; the bridge reads it after each screenshot and the Kindle then does a flashing full refresh. Call it (or `useFullRefresh(key)`) for page-like changes: tab change, modal open/close/switch, screensaver on/off and each screensaver picture. Small toggles and brightness steps stay partial to avoid flashing.
- Share a four-slot action footer across device tabs, with a central physical-controller gap, so actions maintain consistent hardware alignment.
- Keep device action definitions separate from their shared footer rendering and use dedicated device icons, so hardware layout remains consistent across views.
- Language: all visible text goes through `src/lib/i18n.ts` (`t(key)`, one flat dictionary each for `he` and `en`; add every new string to both, a test checks the keys match). The language comes from the server env `UI_LANGUAGE` (default `he`, read per request and delivered in the snapshot result, so a container restart switches it without a rebuild). Hebrew sets `<html lang="he" dir="rtl">`. Entity display names are overridden per language in `NAME_OVERRIDES` in `src/lib/home.ts`.
- RTL rules: wrap numbers and units in `<Ltr>` so they keep reading order. The soft-key footer, the +/- steppers and the media transport use `direction: ltr`, so footer slot 1 (F1) is always bottom-left over the Back button and Minus stays left of Plus. Arrow keys are physical (geometry based); in the pickers ArrowLeft moves to the next item in Hebrew because the grid reads right to left.
- Font: Alef (Regular and Bold, SIL OFL, licence in `src/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`) is bundled and is the primary family for Hebrew and Latin. Never load fonts from the network, the renderer must work offline.
- Focus rule: the arrow keys (5-way) only ever move focus between controls inside `.content` (and never onto the tab bar or footer); tabs change with PageUp/PageDown and the footer fires with F1..F4. The active tab stays highlighted and a pressed soft key shows its footer button as pressed for 120 ms (`flashSoftKey`, shorter than the 200 ms screenshot delay so it never freezes on the panel). Keep new tab or footer markup outside `.content`.
- Main-screen cards (lights, plugs) are display-only: each whole card is one focusable button and Enter opens its full-page modal, which does everything. Do not put inner +/-, toggle or picker buttons on cards. The Power tab has no chart.
- Colour support is per light: `canColor` comes from HA `supported_color_modes` (hs, rgb, rgbw, rgbww, xy). Colour-capable lights get the colour picker on F2 and may show a colour name; other lights get the white-shade picker on F2 and only ever show a shade.
- While the screensaver shows, `<html data-eink-photo="x,y,w,h">` holds the photo box in CSS pixels (viewport 600x800) for the bridge's dithering, and it is removed when the screensaver ends. The photo uses `object-fit: cover`. If Immich photos appear rotated, check EXIF orientation of the preview image first.
- Keep every page inside 600x800 in Hebrew and English; check overflow after changing type sizes.
- Kindle battery: the bridge injects `window.__kindleBattery` and fires a `kindle-battery` event; `useKindleBattery()` in `src/lib/battery.ts` reads both. The badge shows at the end of the tab bar (hidden while charging) and on the screensaver (charging glyph instead of the percentage); below 15 % it is inverted and bold. Nothing shows until the first report. Updates never request a full refresh.
- Screensaver photos are cropped server-side with `sharp` (`src/lib/photo.server.ts`): favorites are picked with portrait photos weighted 3:1 (EXIF orientation 5 to 8 swaps width and height), never the same photo twice in a row, then rotated, cover-cropped to exactly 600x800 around the most interesting area, converted to grayscale and sent as a JPEG data URL. If cropping fails the uncropped preview is sent; if Immich is unavailable the bundled sample photo is shown.
- Voice bridge (`src/routes/api/voice.$.ts`, helpers in `src/lib/voice-match.ts`): Home Assistant Assist reaches the app on `127.0.0.1:8791` (the app itself; `:8790` is the Kindle bridge) under `/api/voice/*` with bearer `VOICE_TOKEN`. It only calls the existing media.server functions (`playSelection`/`startQueue`, `performMediaAction`, `startPowerOn`, `tuneRadio`), so the Kindle screens and the voice assistant share one queue and one power-on job. `GET /api/voice/art?station=<id>` is the one token-less call (a catalog station's art as a colour PNG for Home Assistant's image entity; the Kindle gets the same picture in grayscale via `/media/img`). Station art chain (`src/lib/station-art.ts`, wired in `media.server.ts`): the catalogue `logo` only when it decodes as an image, else a deterministic cover-forge cover (`COVER_FORGE_URL`/`COVER_FORGE_TOKEN`, title = station, artist = band label, cat = FM frequency), else nothing — `artUrl` in the voice answers is set only when a picture really resolved, so HA falls back to its placeholder instead of a broken image. Keep it additive; zod schemas there are built per request because the UI tests import the route tree under vitest-jsdom. After `docker compose up -d --build app`, recreate the bridge too (`docker compose up -d --force-recreate --no-deps bridge`): it shares the app's network namespace and is otherwise left in the old one.
- Queue transport discipline (`src/lib/queue-transport.ts`, wired in `media.server.ts`): every DLNA call to the amp goes through one `TransportLock` (never two in flight); `next`/`previous`/a playlist row move the queue index at once but the renderer call is debounced 700 ms and pushes the latest target; `pushTrack` waits up to 8 s for the renderer to report PLAYING and leaves the clock frozen (`startedAt: null`) when it does not. The stall watchdog in `verifyQueue` freezes the clock when the renderer is idle with time left, replays the track after 6 s (backoff 5/10/20 s), skips a track that never starts (the R-N500 refuses ogg transcodes and WMA; max 3 in a row) and finally pauses with `queue.stall = "renderer stopped"` — the queue must never show "playing" while the amp is idle. `resume` on a queue that claims to play re-sends play when the renderer is not PLAYING. Keep the "amp moved … following" / "clock off … resync" verification intact.
- Amp power on the Media tab (`src/lib/amp-state.ts`, wired in `overlayAmpStatus` / `runPowerOn`): the XML Basic_Status decides power when it answers (Standby = off, over whatever HA says), its last good answer counts for 10 s, the plug off means off, then HA, else unknown — shown as off, never "on" from a stale source. The snapshot keeps a 60 s idle poll while the tab is away and refetches the moment the tab is shown. Footer rule (owner's): while the amp is off, in standby or unknown, the Media tab's F3 reads "Power on" (same action: amp view + the power-on routine) and the amp view's F4 is "Power on"; while the amp is on both footers stay exactly as they are — no standby key. The card shows the routine's step / error while it runs.
- Battery saver (`docs/battery-saver-plan.md`): the Kindle **never suspends** (a button cannot wake a suspended Kindle 4, only the power slider can — owner's test 2026-10-15, so RTC suspend is rejected). While the screensaver shows, `<html data-eink-saver="1">` is set and the bridge's `/schedule` tells the device it may switch its **Wi-Fi radio** off until the next scheduled update (day every `DAY_WAKE_MIN`, night `NIGHT_START`–`NIGHT_END` every `NIGHT_WAKE_MIN`, default 00:00–06:00 every two hours, computed server-side in `SLEEP_TZ` — the device clock is UTC). Inside the night window `getScreensaver` serves the same photo again (no rotation; F1/F4 pass `force` and still get a new one); the photo rotation period is `DAY_WAKE_MIN` (`SnapshotResult.photoMinutes`) and the full-refresh hint follows the photo id, not the fetch time. There is **no sleep badge**: the device is awake and responsive, so nothing on the screensaver may claim otherwise — do not add an "offline"/"sleeping" indicator. Keep the screensaver rule itself unchanged: the saver, not the power logic, decides when the panel is "in use".
